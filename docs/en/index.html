5. Methodology, Presumption of Innocence & Cryptographic Verification

Exact Binomial Model & BH FDR: Within every stratum (court × department × case type × year), each judge's baseline allocation share s_j is measured directly from assigned cases. Parties representing >40% of a stratum are excluded as structural monopolists. One-sided exact binomial probabilities P(X ≥ k | n_P, s_j) are corrected across all 62,166 tests via Benjamini–Hochberg FDR. RED flags satisfy q < 0.01, k ≥ 5, and ratio ≥ 3.0.

Legal Disclaimer & GDPR: Red/Amber indicators are purely statistical triage signals warranting institutional inspection under Art. 9 of the Bulgarian Judiciary Act (random case allocation); they do not assert guilt or unlawful conduct.
